About

Kia ora!

I'm a second-year Electrical and Electronics Engineering undergraduate at the University of Canterbury. My journey with engineering started with curiosity in the garage, where I tinkered alongside my father from a young age. That early curiosity has grown into the skills I'm most proud of today, particularly power electronics and embedded systems. Throughout my studies, I've become increasingly fascinated by how engineering theory applies to real-world challenges, especially within aerospace, power systems, and hardware design.

The practical experience I've gained through university extracurriculars and a placement at the Robinson Institute during high school has given me valuable insight into what it takes to be an effective team member. While I'm comfortable working independently, I thoroughly enjoy learning from others. I’m motivated to work on meaningful projects alongside people who share my enthusiasm, contributing to work that makes a real impact on the community.

Outside of my studies, I love to work on my motorcycle as being in the garage is one of my favourite ways to challenge myself. I really enjoy the chance to learn something outside my usual field, and most importantly, I love being a part of a team where everyone contributes in their own way. :)
